## 1. The business

**The Aurelia Grand** is a 150-room luxury hotel owned by the fictional **Aurelia Hospitality Group**. It has five room segments, a restaurant and bar, a spa, meetings and events, and ancillary income. The group is considering a second property, the fictional 80-room **Aurelia Oasis Resort**, modelled with the same engine.

Base case, model year 1 (FY2027): occupancy 77.0%, ADR SAR 1,153, RevPAR SAR 888, total revenue SAR 93.2m, GOP margin 33.2%, EBITDA SAR 23.2m, closing cash SAR 26.1m.

## 3. The engine

One calculation graph (`aurelia/model/engine.py`) of 13 nodes produces every number in the app. Each node declares the line items it reads and produces, with a plain-language formula, and the graph is evaluated in dependency order once per scenario. Room demand is simulated daily; everything else monthly over 36 months. Results are cached by the exact assumption set, and scenarios store complete assumption trees (including the random seed), so a scenario always reproduces exactly. See [CALCULATION_REFERENCE_GENERATED.md](CALCULATION_REFERENCE_GENERATED.md) for every formula and [CALCULATIONS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md](CALCULATIONS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md) for conventions.
