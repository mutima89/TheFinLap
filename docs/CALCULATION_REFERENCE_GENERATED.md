## 3. KPI definitions

| KPI | Formula | Unit | Better when | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Occupancy | Occupied rooms ÷ Available rooms (after out-of-order) | pct | context | Share of sellable rooms that were occupied. High occupancy at a low rate can destroy value. |
| ADR (gross) | Gross room revenue ÷ Occupied rooms | currency | context | Average daily rate actually achieved per occupied room, before distribution costs, excluding VAT. |
| Net ADR | (Gross room revenue − Commissions & distribution fees) ÷ Occupied rooms | currency | higher better | Rate the hotel keeps after paying OTAs, wholesalers' channels and booking fees. |
| RevPAR | Gross room revenue ÷ Available rooms  (= Occupancy × ADR) | currency | higher better | Room revenue per available room. Combines rate and occupancy. |
| Net RevPAR | Net room revenue ÷ Available rooms | currency | higher better | RevPAR after distribution costs. |
| TRevPAR | Total operating revenue ÷ Available rooms | currency | higher better | Total revenue (all departments) per available room. |
| GOPPAR | Gross operating profit ÷ Available rooms | currency | higher better | Profitability per available room; captures cost efficiency as well as revenue. |
| GOP margin | Gross operating profit ÷ Total operating revenue | pct | higher better | Share of revenue left after departmental and undistributed expenses. |
| EBITDA | GOP − Management fees − Fixed charges | currency | higher better | Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA ÷ Total operating revenue | pct | higher better | EBITDA as a share of revenue. |
| Payroll % of revenue | Total payroll & related ÷ Total operating revenue | pct | lower better | Labour productivity indicator. Must be read alongside activity levels. |
| F&B cost of sales % | F&B cost of sales ÷ F&B revenue | pct | lower better | Food and beverage material cost ratio. |
| Spa utilisation | Treatments delivered ÷ Treatment capacity | pct | context | How much therapist capacity was sold. |
| OTA share of room nights | OTA room nights ÷ Total room nights | pct | context | Dependence on online travel agents. Higher share usually means higher distribution cost. |
| Cash conversion | Operating cash flow ÷ EBITDA | pct | higher better | How much of EBITDA turns into operating cash in the period. |
| Days sales outstanding | Accounts receivable ÷ (Billings incl. VAT) × days | number | lower better | How long customers take to pay. |
| Cancellation rate | Cancelled bookings ÷ Gross bookings accepted | pct | lower better | Share of accepted bookings that cancelled before arrival. |
